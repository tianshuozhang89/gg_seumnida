🧳 The Airport Vincita Luggage Storage Strategy (Pure Freedom)

Instead of hauling empty, bulky bike travel bags around the island each day, leverage Jeju International Airport’s dedicated left-luggage service:

  • Step 1 (Day 1 Arrival · 07:25 AM): Unfold your Bromptons in 15 seconds outside the arrivals hall.
  • Step 2 (Store Vincita Bags at CJU Airport): Head to the CJU Airport Luggage Storage Desk (ZimCarry / 짐보관소, 1F near Gate 5). Check in your empty Vincita Brompton travel bags for 5 days of secure holding (~$7–$8 SGD / 6,000–8,000 KRW per day).
  • Step 3 (Cycle Directly Away): Mount your Bromptons and pedal directly out of the airport terminal onto the coastal road with zero baggage anchor!
  • Step 4 (Luggage Delivery for Clothes): Send your standard clothing suitcases from hotel to hotel each day using ZimCarry / Gabang-i-man (~$15 SGD/day).
  • Step 5 (Day 6 Departure Morning): Take a 10-minute taxi from the Sheraton Jeju Hotel to CJU Airport, retrieve your Vincita travel bags from the 1F storage counter, fold your Bromptons inside in 2 minutes, and roll up to the Scoot check-in counter!
💡 Essential Pre-Trip Briefing
  • The Brompton Advantage: Zero bike wrenching! At seaside cafes and restaurants, simply fold your Bromptons and roll them inside or park them next to your table with zero theft concerns.
  • Navigation Rules: Follow the continuous blue line painted on the coastal tarmac. Always ride counter-clockwise to keep the ocean immediately on your right.
  • Immigration & Entry: Eligible passport holders enjoy 90 days visa-free entry with K-ETA exemption. Submit the free electronic arrival card (e-Arrival Card at www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr) online within 72 hours before departure.
  • August Climate: Warm coastal summer (27°C–31°C). Bring high-SPF sunblock, light summer cycling kit, and wind vest for early morning departures.
